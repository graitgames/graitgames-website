Introduction

Welcome to GRaiT Games (graitgames.com). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how we handle your information.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. By visiting our website or subscribing to our newsletter, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We keep things simple. Here's what we collect and why:

  • Email addresses — only when you voluntarily sign up for our newsletter.
  • Usage data — anonymous browsing data (pages visited, time on site, device type) collected automatically via Google Analytics.
  • Technical data — IP addresses and request logs processed by Cloudflare solely for security and performance purposes.

We do not collect names, physical addresses, payment information, or any other personal details. We have no user accounts on this website.

How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used for one purpose only:

  • Sending you our newsletter — updates, game news, and announcements from GRaiT Games.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your email address to any third party for marketing purposes. We use anonymous analytics data solely to understand how visitors use our site and to improve it.

Cookie Policy

Our website uses cookies in the following limited ways:

  • Analytics cookies — set by Google Analytics to distinguish unique visitors and track sessions anonymously.
  • Security cookies — set by Cloudflare to identify trusted web traffic and prevent abuse.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any cookies for targeted advertising. You can control or disable cookies via your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

Children's Privacy

GRaiT Games is a gaming website and we take children's privacy seriously. Our website is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 14 in applicable jurisdictions).

Because we have no user accounts, the only personal data we could ever collect from a child is an email address submitted to our newsletter. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted their email to us, please contact us immediately at support@graitgames.com and we will promptly delete it.

Unsubscribe & Data Deletion

You are always in control of your data. Here's how to exercise your rights:

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter — every email we send includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it and you're done.
  • Request deletion of your data — email us at support@graitgames.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and we will permanently remove your information from our systems and from MailerLite within 30 days.
  • Access your data — you may also request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.

As a Canadian website, we respect your rights under PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act).
