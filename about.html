// Our Mission //

Building games, learning together.

GRaiT GAMES is a father-and-son workshop where we design, code, and publish browser-based games — and share everything we learn along the way. Our mission is simple: make learning to code feel like play, and prove that anyone, at any age, can build something worth playing.

// Our Story //

How it all started

It began at the kitchen table with a simple question: "Dad, how are video games made?" Instead of just answering, we decided to find out together. What started as a weekend curiosity turned into an ongoing adventure in creativity, problem-solving, and a whole lot of trial and error.

  1. The Big Question

    A curious kid, a patient parent, and a laptop. We started with tiny experiments — moving a square across the screen — and celebrated every small win like a championship trophy.

  2. Learning the Building Blocks

    HTML, CSS, and a sprinkle of JavaScript. We learned that code is just a set of clear instructions, and that mistakes aren't failures — they're clues that help you get better.

  3. Our First Real Game

    The first time something we built actually played back, we knew we were hooked. It wasn't perfect, but it was ours — and that feeling is exactly what we want to pass on.

  4. Sharing It With the World

    GRaiT GAMES is the home for everything we create. It's our arcade, our notebook, and our invitation to other families and learners to start their own coding adventure.

// AI-Assisted Development //

Built with a little help from AI

We believe in being open about how we build. AI tools are part of our workshop — think of them as a friendly, always-on tutor sitting beside us. Here's exactly what that looks like:

🤖

A Coding Tutor

AI helps explain new concepts, suggests approaches, and answers the endless "why does this work?" questions — turning confusion into understanding faster.

🔍

A Second Pair of Eyes

When code breaks (and it always does), AI helps us spot bugs and understand error messages, so debugging becomes a lesson instead of a roadblock.

💡

An Idea Spark

From game mechanics to art direction, AI helps us brainstorm and explore possibilities — but the creative decisions are always ours to make.

🎓

Humans Stay in Charge

AI assists; we learn, decide, and build. Every game ships because we understood it, tested it, and were proud enough to press "publish".
